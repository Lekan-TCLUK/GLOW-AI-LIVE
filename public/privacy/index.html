Your photo is submitted to GLOW AI’s analysis service so the system can assess visible appearance characteristics across Radiance, Hydration Appearance, Evenness, Texture, Under-Eye Appearance and Overall Vitality.

GLOW AI does not use your selfie to create a persistent facial profile, facial recognition profile or account-level facial history. The GLOW AI application database stores the resulting assessment data rather than the submitted image.
